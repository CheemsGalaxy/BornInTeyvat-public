## 7. Material not included

The following are intentionally absent from this repository:

- character avatars and PNG files;
- official or fan artwork, key art, logos, and icons;
- constellation names and descriptions;
- character stories, voice lines, and other narrative text;
- the `inspiration` field from the source project's nation records;
- website source code, React components, TypeScript logic, CSS, UI, and design;
- website metadata, Open Graph images, and branding assets;
- third-party database exports, tables, or scraped page content.
